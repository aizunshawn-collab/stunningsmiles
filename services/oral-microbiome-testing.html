Oral Microbiome Testing

Understanding the bacteria living in your mouth — and what it means for your whole-body health.

What Is Oral Microbiome Testing?

Your mouth is home to hundreds of species of bacteria, and the balance between them says a lot about your risk for cavities, gum disease, and even certain whole-body health conditions. Oral microbiome testing analyzes a simple saliva sample to identify which bacteria are present and in what amounts — giving us a far more precise picture than a visual exam alone.

As part of our holistic approach, we use this information to personalize your care — recommending specific preventive steps, products, or treatments based on your actual bacterial profile, not a one-size-fits-all protocol.

How Microbiome Testing Helps Your Care

Saliva-Based Testing

A quick, non-invasive saliva sample collected right in our office — no needles, no discomfort.

Personalized Risk Insights

Results show your individual risk for cavities and gum disease based on your specific bacterial balance.

Guides Treatment Planning

We use your results to recommend targeted rinses, products, or treatment frequency — not generic advice.

Supports Whole-Body Health

Oral bacteria are linked to systemic inflammation, so understanding your profile supports more than just your teeth.

What to Expect at Your Visit

  1. Simple sample collection — A quick, painless saliva swab taken during your regular appointment.
  2. Lab analysis — Your sample is analyzed to identify bacterial species and levels present in your mouth.
  3. Results review — We walk you through your results in plain language, not just raw lab data.
  4. Personalized recommendations — A tailored plan for products, treatments, or follow-up care based on your specific profile.
