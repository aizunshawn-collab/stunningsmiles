Prosthodontics

Rebuilding how your bite looks, feels, and functions — one tooth or a full smile at a time.

What Is Prosthodontics?

Prosthodontics is the branch of dentistry focused on replacing missing or damaged teeth and restoring the way your mouth actually works — biting, chewing, and speaking comfortably again. Whether you're missing one tooth or need a full-arch solution, prosthodontic treatment is built around function first, with a natural appearance built in.

At Stunning Smiles, we design every restoration — denture, bridge, or implant-supported prosthesis — to match your bite, facial structure, and the rest of your natural teeth, using biocompatible materials wherever possible.

Our Prosthodontic Services

Full & Partial Dentures

Custom-fitted removable dentures that restore your smile and bite when several or all teeth are missing.

Fixed Bridges

A permanent, non-removable way to fill the gap left by one or more missing teeth using the neighboring teeth for support.

Implant-Supported Restorations

Dentures and bridges anchored to dental implants for a more stable, natural-feeling result than traditional options alone.

Custom Bite Design

Every restoration is designed around your unique bite so chewing feels natural and your jaw joints stay comfortable.

What to Expect at Your Visit

  1. Consultation & exam — We evaluate your remaining teeth, gums, and jawbone to determine the best restorative option.
  2. Treatment planning — We walk through the options — dentures, bridges, or implant-supported restorations — along with cost and timeline.
  3. Impressions & fabrication — Precise impressions or digital scans are used to custom-build your restoration.
  4. Fitting & adjustment — We fine-tune fit and bite until your restoration feels natural and comfortable.
  5. Follow-up care — Ongoing checkups to make sure your restoration continues to fit and function well over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

It depends on how many teeth need replacing, the health of your remaining teeth and jawbone, and your budget. We'll walk through the realistic options for your specific situation during your consultation.

Implant-supported restorations generally feel most like natural teeth since they're anchored directly to your jawbone, but traditional dentures and bridges remain excellent, more affordable options for many patients.

With proper care, bridges and implant-supported restorations can last well over a decade, and dentures are typically relined or replaced every 5–10 years as your mouth naturally changes shape.
