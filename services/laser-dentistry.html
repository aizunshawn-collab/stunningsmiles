Laser Dentistry

Gentle, precise treatment — often with less discomfort and faster healing than traditional instruments.

What Is Laser Dentistry?

Dental lasers use focused light energy to treat soft and hard tissue with remarkable precision. For many procedures — reshaping gum tissue, treating early cavities, or releasing a tight frenulum — lasers can replace the drill or scalpel entirely, often with less bleeding, less swelling, and a shorter recovery.

As part of our holistic, low-toxin approach, we use laser technology whenever it's the gentler option for the job. It fits naturally with our philosophy: effective treatment with the least possible disruption to your body.

What We Use Laser Dentistry For

Soft Tissue Procedures

Gum reshaping, frenectomies, and treatment of cold sores or canker sores — often completed with little to no bleeding.

Cavity Treatment

Lasers can remove decay and prepare a tooth for a filling, frequently with less need for anesthesia than traditional drilling.

Faster Healing

Laser treatment seals tissue as it works, which typically means less post-procedure swelling and a quicker return to normal.

Reduced Anxiety

No drill noise or vibration for many procedures — a welcome difference for patients with dental anxiety.

What to Expect at Your Visit

  1. Evaluation — We determine whether your procedure is a good candidate for laser treatment.
  2. Comfort measures — Depending on the procedure, little to no anesthesia may be needed.
  3. Laser treatment — Precise, targeted treatment of the affected tissue.
  4. Same-day recovery — Most patients return to normal activities the same day with minimal restrictions.
